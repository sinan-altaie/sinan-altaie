<h1>Hi, I'm Sinan (pronounced "See-None"). I build security programs that hold up under pressure.</h1>

Cybersecurity leader with 20+ years in technology and 6+ years in security-focused roles. I run cybersecurity for Master Electronics, a global electronics distributor with six operating companies and five e-commerce brands. I joined in 2023 as the company's first dedicated security hire and grew the program from one analyst into a five-person team across the United States and Jordan.

What I own today: security strategy and budget, 24x7 detection and response, incident command, vulnerability and attack-surface management, email and identity security, and the governance, risk, and compliance portfolio (ISO 27001, CMMC 2.0, SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR).

Before security I spent a decade running mission-critical data systems for the United Nations and USAID in Baghdad, including the UN mission's country-wide human rights database. That is where I learned to keep systems alive under pressure and to brief leadership with numbers they can act on.

<h2>🛡️ Selected work</h2>

- **A governed agentic SOC pipeline.** Designed and built an autonomous triage and response loop on an existing detection stack, constrained by a deterministic policy engine: reversible-only actions, blast-radius caps, a circuit breaker, a kill switch, and a tamper-evident audit trail. Autonomy was earned through a live red-team tabletop and a formal promotion gate, then moved to 24x7 cloud operation under change control.
- **CMMC 2.0 certification and a public Trust Center.** Gap analysis to certification in seven months, then renewal. Launched the ISO 27001:2022 ISMS, the PCI DSS 4.0.1 program through its first internal audit, and a GDPR gap assessment with outside counsel.
- **Incident command.** Incident commander for every significant event since 2024: containment decisions, external DFIR engagement, executive reporting inside the first day, insurer and counsel coordination, and the remediation programs that follow.
- **Email security platform replacement in three months** across two Microsoft 365 tenants, with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC at enforcement on every brand domain, then MFA, Conditional Access, and the move toward passkeys.
- **Team and human-risk program.** Hired and developed the team, created a Cyber Awareness Lead role, and run monthly phishing simulations, mandatory AI security training, and company-wide security all-hands.
